GameWithUs – Site Plan

Site Name

GameWithUs

A name that invites players to discover and share new gaming experiences together.

Site Purpose

GameWithUs is a hub for the year’s most popular games—organized by genre and community submissions—so players can easily find fresh titles to play next.

Site Overview

  • Home Page: Hero banner + tagline; showcases top 3 games of the year; “Explore” button; brief purpose text box.
  • Explore Page: Sidebar filters (Competitive, RPG, MMO, FPS, etc.); main area toggles between list/grid views.
  • Community Submissions: Form where users can upload upcoming games for the community spotlight.

Scenarios

  • “I just finished an RPG—what other role‑playing games are trending right now?”
  • “I have an indie title—how do I submit it so fellow gamers can discover it?”

Color Schema

  • #010101 (Black): Body text, primary nav backgrounds.
  • #FEFDFD (Soft Peach): Page background & content wrappers.
  • #5F43B2 (Studio): Headings, buttons, link accents.
  • #B1AEBB (Spun Pearl): Section backgrounds, hover states.
  • #3A3153 (Martinique): Subheadings, borders, secondary accents.

Typography

  • Press Start 2P (Headings): A pixel/art‑style font that captures classic gaming vibes.
  • Roboto (Body): A clean, highly legible sans‑serif for descriptions and lists.
