NP Membership Benefits

  • Free basic directory listing on our website
  • Nonprofit event promotion via chamber channels
  • Access to quarterly nonprofit-only networking calls
  • Invitation to volunteer board meetings
  • Discounted rates on training workshops
  • Inclusion in our annual “Community Impact” report

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Everything in NP Membership, plus:
  • Basic spotlight advertisement on home page (rotating banner)
  • 20% discount on all chamber-hosted training sessions
  • Quarterly email feature in our member newsletter
  • Access to member-only webinars and skill-building events
  • Opportunity to sponsor one community luncheon per year

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze perks, plus:
  • Featured directory placement (top of category listings)
  • 30% discount on advertising packages
  • Two complimentary tickets to chamber luncheons each quarter
  • Social media shout-outs (up to 4 posts per year)
  • Priority registration for chamber conferences and expos
  • Access to leadership roundtable discussions

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver perks, plus:
  • Premium website banner ad (fixed position) for one year
  • VIP networking reception invitations (unlimited)
  • Custom sponsorship opportunities at flagship events
  • Dedicated chamber-led social media campaign
  • Complimentary exhibit space at annual business expo
  • Personalized business consultation with chamber advisors
